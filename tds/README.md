## Practical Accountant Workflow

A typical TDS compliance process may involve:

1. Identifying the nature of payment
2. Checking the applicable TDS provision
3. Checking threshold and applicable rate
4. Deducting TDS where applicable
5. Recording the accounting entry
6. Depositing TDS within the applicable timeline
7. Preparing and filing the relevant TDS return
8. Reconciling TDS records
9. Checking Form 26AS / AIS / TRACES information where relevant

## Important

TDS provisions, rates, thresholds, forms, and compliance requirements may change.

Always verify the applicable provisions and rates for the relevant financial year and transaction before taking compliance action.
