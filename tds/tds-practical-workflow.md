# TDS Practical Workflow for Accountants

## Objective

This guide explains a practical workflow that an accountant can follow while handling TDS-related work.

The exact provision, rate, threshold, form, and due date should always be verified for the relevant financial year and transaction.

---

## Step 1 – Identify the Nature of Payment

First identify what the payment relates to.

Examples:

- Salary
- Professional fees
- Contractor payment
- Rent
- Commission
- Interest
- Other applicable payments

---

## Step 2 – Identify the Applicable TDS Provision

After identifying the nature of payment, determine the applicable TDS provision under the law applicable to the relevant period.

---

## Step 3 – Check Threshold and Rate

Before deduction, verify:

- Applicable threshold
- Applicable TDS rate
- PAN availability
- Applicable conditions or exceptions
- Relevant financial year

---

## Step 4 – Calculate TDS

Determine the amount of TDS required to be deducted based on the applicable rules.

### Example

Professional fee:

₹50,000

Assume an illustrative TDS rate of 10%.

TDS:

₹5,000

Net payment:

₹45,000

> This is only an illustrative example. The actual rate and applicability must be verified for the relevant transaction and financial year.

---

## Step 5 – Pass Accounting Entry

A typical accounting structure may be:

Professional Fees A/c Dr.  
    To TDS Payable A/c  
    To Vendor / Bank A/c

The exact entry depends on the transaction and accounting treatment.

---

## Step 6 – Deposit TDS

The deducted TDS should be deposited within the applicable statutory timeline.

Always verify the due date applicable to the relevant period.

---

## Step 7 – Maintain Records

Maintain appropriate records such as:

- Vendor details
- PAN
- Nature of payment
- Invoice/payment details
- TDS calculation
- TDS deducted
- TDS deposited
- Challan details

---

## Step 8 – TDS Return

Prepare and file the applicable TDS statement/return for the relevant period.

Verify:

- PAN
- Amount paid/credited
- TDS deducted
- TDS deposited
- Challan details
- Deductee details

---

## Step 9 – Reconciliation

Reconcile:

**Books → TDS Records → Challans → TDS Return → Available tax records**

Investigate any differences before finalizing the records.

---

## Common TDS Mistakes

- Wrong section/provision
- Wrong rate
- Ignoring threshold
- Incorrect PAN
- Wrong deductee details
- Incorrect challan details
- Difference between books and TDS return
- Late deduction
- Late deposit
- Incorrect reporting

---

## Accountant Checklist

- [ ] Rate verified
- [ ] PAN checked

- [ ] TDS deposited
- [ ] Return prepared
