# GSTR-1 – Practical Guide for Accountants

## What is GSTR-1?

GSTR-1 is a GST return/report used to furnish details of outward supplies made by a registered taxpayer.

It mainly contains details of sales and other outward supplies.

## What Information is Reported?

Common information includes:

- B2B invoices
- B2C supplies
- Export invoices
- Credit notes
- Debit notes
- Amendments
- Taxable value
- CGST
- SGST
- IGST
- HSN/SAC related details

## Important B2B Information

For B2B invoices, an accountant generally needs to check:

- Customer GSTIN
- Customer name
- Invoice number
- Invoice date
- Taxable value
- Place of supply
- Tax rate
- CGST
- SGST
- IGST
- Reverse charge applicability

## Practical Accountant Workflow

### Step 1 – Collect Sales Data

Collect the sales register for the relevant tax period.

### Step 2 – Check Invoice Details

Verify:

- Invoice number
- Invoice date
- Customer GSTIN
- Taxable value
- GST rate
- Tax amount
- Place of supply

### Step 3 – Classify Transactions

Separate transactions into appropriate categories such as:

- B2B
- B2C
- Export
- Credit Note
- Debit Note
- Other applicable outward supplies

### Step 4 – Reconcile With Books

Compare the GST data with the accounting records.

### Step 5 – Prepare GSTR-1 Data

Prepare the required information according to the applicable GST return tables.

### Step 6 – Review

Check totals and important invoice-level details before filing.

### Step 7 – File / Submit

Complete the applicable filing process on the GST portal after proper review and authorization.

## Common Mistakes to Check

- Incorrect GSTIN
- Wrong invoice number
- Wrong invoice date
- Incorrect tax rate
- Incorrect place of supply
- Missing invoices
- Duplicate invoices
- Incorrect credit/debit notes
- Difference between books and GST data

## Accountant Checklist

- [ ] Sales register checked
- [ ] Invoice sequence checked
- [ ] GSTIN verified
- [ ] Taxable value reconciled
- [ ] GST amount reconciled
- [ ] Credit/debit notes checked
- [ ] Export invoices checked
- [ ] Books vs GST data reconciled
- [ ] Final review completed
