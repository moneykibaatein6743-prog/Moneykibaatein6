# Accounts Payable Ageing Analysis Project

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates a practical Accounts Payable (AP) Ageing Analysis process used by accountants and finance teams to monitor outstanding vendor liabilities.

The project uses fictional data for learning and portfolio purposes.

## Objective

The objective is to analyse outstanding vendor invoices based on their ageing and payment status.

## Ageing Categories

Common ageing buckets include:

* Current / Not Due
* 1–30 Days
* 31–60 Days
* 61–90 Days
* 91–180 Days
* Above 180 Days

## Practical Workflow

### Step 1 – Collect Vendor Outstanding Data

Obtain the vendor payable report or ledger from the accounting system.

### Step 2 – Verify Invoice Details

Review:

* Vendor name
* Invoice number
* Invoice date
* Due date
* Invoice amount
* TDS
* Amount paid
* Outstanding amount

### Step 3 – Calculate Ageing

Calculate the number of days between the invoice due date and the reporting date.

### Step 4 – Classify Outstanding

Classify invoices into appropriate ageing buckets.

### Step 5 – Analyse Vendor Balances

Identify:

* High-value outstanding invoices
* Overdue invoices
* Long-pending balances
* Payments due shortly
* Vendors requiring confirmation

### Step 6 – Prepare AP MIS

Prepare a summary showing:

* Total outstanding
* Current / Not Due
* 1–30 days
* 31–60 days
* 61–90 days
* 91–180 days
* Above 180 days
